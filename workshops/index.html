Workshops taught

Since 2022 I have taught workshops on quantitative methods, data manipulation and data visualisation in R for MA and PhD students and for researchers in linguistics, the social sciences and peace and conflict research. They last from a single afternoon to a full week and can be adapted to the group’s level, from first steps in R to advanced modelling.

  • University of Verona, Department of Cultures and Civilizations (2023, 2024): statistics, data manipulation and visualisation in R, for MA and PhD students.
  • Konstanz Methods Excellence Workshops (KOMEX), Universität Konstanz (2024, 2025): data visualisation with ggplot2, co-taught.
  • Peace Research Institute Frankfurt (PRIF) (2025): introduction to data visualisation in R with ggplot2.
  • Department of Linguistics, Universität Konstanz: regular sessions on quantitative and experimental methods.

The full list is in my CV (PDF).

Arranging a workshop

To arrange a workshop for your group, write to me at massimiliano.canzi@uni-konstanz.de with the topic, the group’s size and background, and possible dates.
